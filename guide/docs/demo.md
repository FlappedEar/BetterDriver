# Try it without a track

Two demo receivers let you try the app without a car or a track day. Their positions are not
real data, and their sessions never count as evidence for the receiver checks.

Add them in **Settings › Equipment › Add equipment**, in the **Try the app** group at the bottom.

## Demo track

Drives a made-up car around a track, so the track screen, lap timing, sectors, the predicted lap,
the live coach and the measured headset delay all run on the phone, anywhere. Put the headset on
to hear the coach.

Open the Demo track item in **Settings › Equipment** and pick what it drives with **Drives**:

- **Demo circuit**: a made-up 2.1 km circuit. The made-up driver brakes too early or too slowly
  now and then, so the coach has something to say after about five minutes.
- **Built-in laps** from real track days (Silesia Ring and Autodrom Jastrząb), replayed one lap
  after another at their saved track.
- **One of your own sessions**: its timed laps are replayed at its saved track. A session needs a
  saved track and at least two timed laps at pace; otherwise the Demo circuit drives instead.

Choosing a drive saves its track on the phone.

## Demo GNSS

Made-up positions on a circle, for trying recording and the screens without a receiver.

## Import a session to review

To try the analysis with real laps, import a VBO, GPX or RaceChrono CSV file with
**Sessions › Import session**. See [Sessions](sessions.md#import-a-session).
