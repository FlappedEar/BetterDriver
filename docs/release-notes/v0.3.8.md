Alpha 0.3.8: a realistic optimal lap, one clear focus for the next session, and steadier corner detection.

What changes for the driver:
- **Realistic optimal lap (FBBD-178).** The summary shows three times, each named for what it is:
  - **Best actual lap:** your fastest real lap.
  - **Realistic optimal:** the fastest lap you have shown you can combine. Segments from different laps are joined only where the two laps match at the join: speed within 3 km/h, line within 1.5 m, heading within 0.05 rad, longitudinal g within 0.25 g, and never braking against accelerating.
  - **Raw theoretical best:** the sum of each segment's fastest time.
  The composite video now plays the realistic optimal by default, and the raw theoretical best is still available. On the Silesia Ring recording the three times are 142.21 s, 139.43 s and 138.01 s.
- **Next session focus (FBBD-20).** The analysis summary opens with one primary focus and at most two secondary ones.
  - Each focus shows the time available and what your faster passes did differently: braking point, minimum speed, exit speed.
  - A number is shown only when every input to it is High confidence, measured and in a known unit; otherwise the change is described in words.
  - At the same track, the next session's coach (at verbosity Focus) speaks about those corners first.
- **Steadier corners (FBBD-215).** Corner count, corner identity, theoretical best and the chosen focus no longer change when the same drive is stored at lower precision, has a little GPS noise, a different sample rate or a few missing fixes. A gap of under 0.5 s between GPS fixes no longer splits a lap or drops its sector times.
- **Track screen kept updating after reopening the app (FBBD-209).** Reopening the app could freeze the track screen on its last state, so a GNSS loss was never shown. The connection to the recorder now stays up while any screen uses it.
- **More reliable release checks (FBBD-208).** Five intermittent emulator test failures were traced to their causes and fixed. No limits were loosened.
