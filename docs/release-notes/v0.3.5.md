What changes for the driver:
- **G-forces fitted to GPS (FBBD-204).**
  - Each accelerometer axis is checked against the acceleration GPS shows. It is turned round if it reads backwards, its tilt is taken off, and GPS is used instead when the two do not agree.
  - Braking on the video overlay now reads as braking with the RaceBox.
  - The live G reads 0.00 g when standing still, instead of 0.19 g from a tilted mount.
  - The Summary, and a session without laps, has a "G-forces · whole session" card with the G-G diagram and peaks. The Silesia mock session now shows real G-forces (from GPS) instead of one dot.
- **Compare laps (FBBD-205).**
  - It always opens on the picker with nothing picked; Back or Change laps returns there with nothing picked.
  - Two laps opened from Corner laps still open compared.
