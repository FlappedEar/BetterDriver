1.17.0: the Car tab is the car's own page, with Scan car and your own OBD-II and CAN parameters (FBBD-344), a single lap's video with data starts and ends a few seconds around the lap like FlappedEar Overlays (FBBD-346), and fixes from the code audit (FBBD-348). Batched so Arek has what is on main.

What changes for the driver:
- The Car tab is the car's own page: live values, **Your parameters** and **What the car offers**. **Scan car** reads which OBD-II values the car reports, its VIN prefix and the CAN ids heard at the OBD port (rate, changing bytes, last frame) and keeps them with the car (#386).
- The OBD-II page lists the values the car reported, each with a switch for whether a session records it; the CAN bus page lists the ids heard and names the ones already read (#386).
- **Add parameter** in three steps: a CAN id, or an OBD request such as `22 F190`; a RaceChrono-style formula checked against the scanned frame or tried live on the car; then name and unit. Your parameters are recorded in every session with that car (#386).
- A lap's video with data starts 5 to 8 s before its start/finish line and ends as long after (6 s to start with, set under **Seconds before and after the lap**). The overlay shows 0:00 until the line, runs during the lap, then holds the final time and the gap to the best lap (#394).
- Lining up GoPro files by GPS searches only the time the camera could overlap (at most 6 h, offsets within 12 h), and a downloaded GoPro file whose MP4 index does not read is downloaded again (#396).
- A backup with an oversized coach or calibration file leaves that file out instead of failing (#396).
- iOS app work on main (TestFlight builds, not part of the Android APK): GoPro start and stop with the session (#390), Wi-Fi OBD-II and safe OBD and heart rate on drops (#388), recovery of interrupted sessions at app start (#396).
