Alpha 0.3.41: steadier coaching on the Demo track's Silesia Ring laps (FBBD-244), and corner cues that say where to brake and what speed to carry (FBBD-242).

What changes for the driver:
- A lap that starts out of the pit lane is coached again. Before, slowing gently on the pit exit was taken for a cool-down, and the coach said nothing until the line.
- After a lap where you lift off to cool down or for traffic, the next lap's advice is based on your last lap at race pace, not on the cruising lap. Before, it could say "carry 10 more" about a corner you rolled through at 30 km/h.
- During a cool-down the coach still gives no advice, but it now says the time of the lap you just finished.
- A brake cue now names the brake point first: "Turn 3, brake at 100, 10 later" (Arek's pick UI-87 C). The number is the metres from the brake point the coach asks for to the start of the corner, the same corner start as Lap analysis › Corner details (UI-89 A). Where the track has no corners marked, or the brake point is already past the corner's start, it is counted to the apex. "Brake earlier" cues read the same way: "Turn 3, brake at 120, 15 earlier".
- A speed cue now names the apex speed to aim for: "Turn 3, 60 at the apex, 5 more" (UI-88 C).
- When the straight before a corner is too short for the whole cue, the coach says the target alone: "Turn 3, brake at 100" or "Turn 3, 60 at the apex".
- The coach line on the track screen shows the same: "Brake at 100 m · 10 later" or "60 km/h at the apex · 5 more".
- Cues below High confidence still go without numbers, as before ("brake a little later").
- Numbers from 100 to 999 are said from the pre-recorded voice clips the way track people say them ("1 50", "1 oh 5", "2 hundred"), and the coach allows for the longer phrase when it decides whether a cue fits before the braking zone.
- The cue review reports against the target: "Braked at 95 m, 10 m later than before" and "Apex 58 km/h, 4 more than before". Cue logs from earlier versions still read as before.
- Recording, lap timing, analysis and video are unchanged from 0.3.40.
