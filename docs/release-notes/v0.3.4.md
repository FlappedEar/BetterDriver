Alpha 0.3.4: the build for the validation drive. The app now keeps one continuous diagnostic log and grades every drive against the hardware targets.

What changes for the driver:
- **One log for every drive (FBBD-193).**
  - The phone keeps a single log across all sessions and app restarts.
  - It is never deleted on its own.
  - Settings › Diagnostics has **Share log** (one zip) and **Clear log**, which asks first and shows the size.
- **Hardware checks from your normal driving.**
  - Each session is graded against the hardware targets: GNSS rate and gaps, the 30-min soak, video sync and drift for each camera, GoPro start/stop, cue timing and delay, jank, screen-off, GNSS-lost banners and export time.
  - The Data quality card shows "n pass · n fail · n not measured" and lists the failures with their Jira keys.
- **More logged.**
  - Every 30 s: frame times and battery temperature, charge and thermal state.
  - Each phone video frame's capture time.
  - For each coach cue: when its audio was ready, the output device and audio focus.
  - GoPro start/stop acknowledgements and import alignment.
  - VBO export timing.
- **Emulator fault tests in CI (FBBD-195).** CI now runs these on the emulator:
  - memory pressure, 2 minutes with the screen off, recorder kill (1.6 s without data, limit 2 s), stopping 10 times in a row
  - Demo GNSS loss and return, 10 cycles
  - denying and revoking permissions
