1.3.1: car data reconnects after a dropped adapter, and stopping a session no longer crashes the recorder (FBBD-269, FBBD-271).

What changes for the driver:
- Stopping a session while the OBD-II adapter was answering a request could crash the recorder process (it restarted at once, and the session was saved). Stopping now ends car data quietly, over Wi-Fi, Bluetooth and Bluetooth LE.
- When the phone's Wi-Fi or Bluetooth comes back during a session, the adapter is tried again at once instead of up to 20 s later. While it runs, a recording also tells Android that it uses a Wi-Fi network without internet, as the adapter's is.
- The app log now says when a Wi-Fi network is lost or available, when Bluetooth goes on or off, and, every minute, that the search for an adapter is still running. On 20 Oct the Wi-Fi adapter dropped at 140 km/h and car data stayed off for 4 minutes; the next log shows whether the phone left the adapter's network.
- Recording, timing, video, coach and storage are otherwise unchanged from 1.3.0.
