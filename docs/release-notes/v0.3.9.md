Alpha 0.3.9: what came of each focus, progress over every session at a track, corner corrections that stick, coasting per corner, every pass through a corner with its video, and the fixes from the 2026-10-13 drive.

What changes for the driver:
- **Coaching history (FBBD-180).** The summary says what came of the last focus: whether the coach worked on it, whether the braking point, minimum or exit speed moved the way it asked, whether the corner got faster, and on how many passes. Earlier gains are marked Learned, Partly held or Lost in later sessions. Numbers are shown only when every pass behind them is High confidence and measured; otherwise the same sentence appears without the number.
- **Progression (FBBD-181).** "Progress over every session here" opens laps (best, typical, spread), every corner's median time, braking point, minimum and exit speed, throttle pickup and repeatability, and the coaching results, per session, track day or month, with a trend from three points on. Filter by period, track day or session. Imported sessions take part and are marked with their origin.
- **Corner corrections (FBBD-179).** In the track editor you can rename, move, split, merge, remove or retype a corner, or restore the automatic corners. Corrections are kept with the track, every session there uses them, untouched corners keep their identity, and progression follows corners across corrections. Backups and shared tracks carry them.
- **Driving states and coasting (FBBD-175).** Compare shows braking, coasting, cornering, accelerating and full throttle along each lap. Corner details show coasting time and distance per corner (≈ when inferred without pedal channels). The summary shows the typical (median) lap above the lap spread, and a track day lists every lap in order with its trend.
- **Hot phone keeps the frame rate (FBBD-21).** When the phone overheats, video now drops resolution before frame rate (4K·60 → 1080p·60 → 720p·60).
- **VBO export for FlappedEar Overlays (FBBD-216).** Exports add longitudinal and lateral G columns that Overlays recognises.
- **Next-session focus files (FBBD-20)** now record their format and version, so a newer file is never misread.
- **Every pass through a corner (FBBD-93).** One tap from a focus corner, a coaching line, a progression corner or corner details opens that corner's passes: every counted pass with speed, brake and throttle traces across the corner, and the video of a pass cued to the corner, or two passes side by side.
- **RaceBox link and video placement from the 2026-10-13 drive (FBBD-218).** The phone asks the RaceBox for a fast link again while streaming and logs the link settings, so messages no longer queue up and get dropped; samples are no longer stamped late when they do. Phone video is placed by the camera's own frame times, which removes the 50–94 ms start error. G-forces pick whichever accelerometer axis agrees with GPS, however the RaceBox is mounted.
- **Local time in file names and logs (FBBD-217).**
