Alpha 0.3.38: the phone records 2.7K at 60 fps, between 4K and 1080p (FBBD-239).

What changes for the driver:
- **Settings › Recording › Video · Phone camera** lists **2.7K · 60** between 4K and 1080p, on a phone whose back camera streams a 16:9 size between 1080p and 4K at 60 fps that its H.264 encoder takes (Arek's picks UI-83 B, UI-84 A). On the Pixel 6 that is 2688×1512, about half of 4K's pixels; its 0.3.36 log shows the camera streams it at 60 fps.
- 2.7K records through its own Camera2 and MediaRecorder path, because CameraX has presets for 720p, 1080p and 4K only. Files, sync with the data, audio, the 4 GB file hand-over without a gap, and the 60 °C battery stop work as for the other modes. If 2.7K cannot start, video falls to 1080p · 60.
- The note under the Video list now says video keeps its mode for the whole session, Android may dim the screen when the phone is hot, and video stops only at a 60 °C battery (UI-85 B). The old note still described heat step-downs, which ended in 0.3.30.
- Each 2.7K file writes "WideRecorder: phone_NNNN.mp4: 2688x1512@60 N frames in S s = F fps B bytes" to the log, so the next drive's log shows the frame rate actually recorded.
- Recording, lap timing, analysis and the coach are unchanged from 0.3.37.
