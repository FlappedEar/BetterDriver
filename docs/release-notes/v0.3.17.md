Alpha 0.3.17: more thermal headroom while recording. The phone asks Android for a steadier, cooler pace while the track screen is shown. Use this build for the next drive and the headset test.

What changes for the driver:
- **Sustained performance mode.** While the track screen is shown the app asks Android to hold the processor and graphics at a speed they can keep up without heating, on phones that offer it. Peaks are lower, so the phone throttles and steps the video down later. Nothing changes on screen.
- **60 Hz display while driving.** On a 90 or 120 Hz screen (a Pixel 6 runs at 90 Hz) the track screen asks for 60 Hz, which still shows every one of the 25 updates a second and draws a third fewer frames. The phone goes back to its own setting when the track screen closes.
- **The log records how hard the phone charges.** Every 30 s health line now carries the battery current. On the 2026-10-14 drive plugging in a nearly empty phone raised the heat forecast from 0.63 to 0.77 in a minute, and the next log shows how much of the heat comes from charging. The debug log also says whether sustained performance mode was granted and which refresh rate was asked for.
