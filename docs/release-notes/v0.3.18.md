Alpha 0.3.18: the headset delay measures itself. There is no tap test any more; connect the helmet headset and the coach measures its delay at the start of every coached session.

What changes for the driver:
- **No headset tap test.** The 40-tap test (Settings › Coach › Headset delay) is gone. At the start of each coached session the coach plays a moment of silence on the output the cues go to and reads Android's audio clock: the sound handed to the headset but not yet heard is its delay. Cues are said that much earlier.
- **Settings › Coach shows the delay.** While the page is open it shows the delay measured just now and on which headset, measured again every 5 s so connecting the headset updates it. The slider stays only as the delay used when Android gives no audio clock for the output.
- **Logged for the 1.0 checks.** Every cue's sound start in the session log carries the delay used and whether it was measured or set (out_delay_src). Each measurement also goes in the continuous log. The drive report's "Total delay to the ear" check (FBBD-49) now computes from that with nothing to run.
