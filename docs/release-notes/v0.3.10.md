Alpha 0.3.10: files and logs in your own time, a steadier RaceBox link, video placed by its frames, G from a real accelerometer, and one tap from a corner to its passes.

What changes for the driver:
- **Local time (FBBD-217).** Session folders, debug log lines, the shared log zip and the CSV export use the phone's time zone; log lines and the CSV carry the UTC offset, so they still name one instant. VBO and GPX stay in UTC, as their formats require, so Circuit Tools and FlappedEar Overlays read them as before.
- **RaceBox link (FBBD-218).** Your 2026-10-13 log showed one message per second lost from minute 9 on, queued in the RaceBox. The app now asks for a fast Bluetooth link when streaming starts and again whenever data falls more than 100 ms behind or messages go missing, and it logs the connection interval. Samples are no longer stamped late during a backlog.
- **Phone video start (FBBD-218).** Phone clips are placed by the camera's own frame times, which removes the 88 ms start error.
- **Accelerometer (FBBD-218).** The phone's accelerometer is now recorded in every session. Analysis works out how each accelerometer sits (turned round, by 90°, on its side or tilted in a holder) from GPS, and uses the RaceBox's first and the phone's when the RaceBox's does not match. Your existing sessions get the RaceBox part when reopened.
- **Sample-rate check.** Passes from 24 Hz, as you set; missing messages are still judged by the dropped-samples check.
- **Corner passes (FBBD-93).** From the next-session focus, the coaching history and the progression's corners, one tap opens every pass through that corner with its speed, brake and throttle traces and its video.
