1.6.1: steadier, faster engine speed and temperatures from the OBD-II adapter, and no CAN listening on the Corolla (FBBD-284).

What changes for the driver:
- The CAN id probe and the steering listens run only on the FR-S/86/BRZ (VIN starting JF1Z, or a car profile named FR-S, GT86, FT86, Toyota 86 or BRZ); on the Corolla they cost no polling time and the log says "can_listen off" (FBBD-284).
- An adapter that answers slowly (Wi-Fi clone, about 230 ms a request) gets a shorter wait for more answers (AT ST 102, 78, then 61 ms); a step that loses an answer goes back to the one before. Simulated clone: rpm 1.78 to 4.3 Hz, temperatures 0.18 to 0.43 Hz (FBBD-284).
- Temperatures that come due together are read one at a time between engine speed readings, so rpm no longer stalls for 0.5 s every 2 s; the fast request no longer drops its response count every 50 requests (Vgate-like longest rpm gap 193 to 145 ms) (FBBD-284).
