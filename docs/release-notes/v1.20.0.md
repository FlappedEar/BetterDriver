What changes for the driver:
- At a place with no saved track, or a track saved without its start/finish, the app finds the loop once it has been driven about one and a half times and puts a provisional start/finish across the middle of its longest straight. The laps so far are timed through it, then laps, sectors and delta go on live on the track screen (#407).
- The provisional line is not saved; after the session, Make a track from this session still offers the track, and a line placed in the track editor wins from the next session (#407).
- The iPhone app does the same from its next TestFlight build (shared timing, #407).
