Alpha 0.3.22: lap analysis opens quickly again (FBBD-228).

What changes for the driver:
- **Lap analysis opens at once again.** Since 0.3.19 the G-forces average the RaceBox and the phone's accelerometer (FBBD-226). The phone records about 110 samples a second, and the analysis compared the times of the phone's three axes again at every one of its samples: tens of billions of comparisons on a half-hour drive, so the screen took ages. The times are now compared once per axis, and the clock matching between the accelerometers and GPS reads its windows in one sweep instead of one search per window. The G-forces come out the same.
- **The debug log says how long each analysis took** ("SessionAnalysis: session ... analysed in N ms"), so a drive log shows a slow screen.
