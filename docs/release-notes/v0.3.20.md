Alpha 0.3.20: fixes from Arek's 2026-10-15 drive log (two road sessions on 0.3.18, 7 and 14 min).

What changes for the driver:
- **GNSS samples line up with the video after a cold start.** When the RaceBox gets its first fix during a session, its time keeps settling onto GNSS time for a few seconds. The app took the Bluetooth delay at the fix and then only let it rise slowly, so on the 13:04 session every sample after the fix was stamped about 0.17 s early against the video. For the first minute after a first fix the delay now follows the receiver freely, then the backlog guard (FBBD-218) comes back on. Sessions that start with a fix (the 13:15 one) were not affected.
- **The validation report no longer misses the end of the video.** The report made at the stop read the video length before the camera had written its final one, and was then kept. The 13:04 session showed video coverage 97.8 % (FAIL) although the video was complete. A report is now made again whenever the video index changed after it was computed.
