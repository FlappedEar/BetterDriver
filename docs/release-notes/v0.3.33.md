Alpha 0.3.33: a Demo track receiver for laps, sectors and coach cues without a track day (FBBD-233).

What changes for the driver:
- **Demo track.** Settings › Devices › + Add equipment › "Laps and coach cues on a made-up circuit" adds a receiver that drives a made-up car around a made-up 2.1 km circuit (#196). Starting it saves the "Demo circuit" track on its own, so the track screen, lap timing, sectors, predicted lap, live coach cues and the measured headset delay all run on the phone anywhere, with the headset on. Its positions are not real data.
- **Report.** A session on Demo GNSS or Demo track shows its GNSS section as NOT MEASURED ("demo receiver"), so it never counts as receiver evidence; its laps, cues and headset delay are judged as on a real track.
- Recording, video, heat and real-receiver behaviour are unchanged from 0.3.32.
