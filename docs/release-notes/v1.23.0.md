1.23.0: the gear on the track screen and in the video, worked out from RPM and speed.

What changes for the driver:
- Gear can now be picked as a track screen tile in Settings › Track screen, in the car's group after RPM (UI-309 A). After about a minute of driving with RPM the session fits the car's gears from its own RPM and speed (on the FR-S, 2nd to 4th of the 6-speed automatic); during a shift, while the converter slips and before the gears are known the tile shows a dash. A car whose engine follows no fixed gears, such as a hybrid, gets no gear, and the tile is left out (FBBD-397).
- Video with data and Watch video show the gear large in the middle of the RPM gauge, with the speed small under it and no RPM number, since the arc shows the engine speed (UI-310 A). A session without RPM shows the speed alone (FBBD-397).
- The iPhone gets both from the same shared code; its Tech HUD painter draws the same gauge (FBBD-397).
