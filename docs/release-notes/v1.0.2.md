What changes for the driver:
- The coach measures the audio output's delay again whenever the output changes during a session, such as a Bluetooth headset connected after the start or the sound switched to another device. Until the new output is measured, the cues use the delay set in the coach settings; once it is measured, the cues are timed for it. On 20 Oct the headset was connected after the start and its cues were timed for the phone speaker's 100 ms.
- The log names the device Android actually sends the coach voice to (Android 13 and later), not just a headset that happens to be connected.
- The drive report has a new check, "Cues with their output's delay measured": every cue must be timed for a delay measured on the output it was said on.
- Lap timing, video and storage are unchanged from 1.0.1.
