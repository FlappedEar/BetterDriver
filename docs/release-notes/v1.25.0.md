1.25.0: the session list can be hidden on a tablet, the gear no longer drops out between shifts, and the iPhone and iPad gain the optimal-lap video, Apple Maps satellite, RaceBox diagnostics and the camera test.

What changes for the driver:
- On a tablet (Android and iPad) a list button at the left of the session's bar hides the session list beside the session, so the analysis takes the whole width, and shows it again; closing the session brings the list back (FBBD-399, #464).
- The Gear tile and the video's gear stay on the gear through the torque converter's slip and change where the RPM steps to the next gear, so they no longer show a dash for seconds under braking and back on the throttle (FBBD-400, #465).
- iPhone and iPad: the realistic optimal and the raw theoretical best can be saved as video from the Video tab, cut from the laps each corner was driven on, as on Android (FBBD-370, #468).
- On iPhone and iPad the track editor opens on Apple Maps satellite imagery (Satellite/Map switch, no key needed), and the perfect lap in 3D shows the satellite photo under the road; a saved 3D video keeps the drawn road (FBBD-381, #467).
- The iPhone gets Android's RaceBox diagnostics (live values, read-only queries, motion-sensor calibration applied to new sessions) and the camera test (1080p · 60, 12 min with MP4 then 12 min without, results in the log), both on the shared core; Android's diagnostics text and camera test log lines now come from the same shared code with the same output (FBBD-371, #471).
- The iPhone log now has the once-a-minute load line while a session records (CPU, memory, threads), the same shared line as Android (FBBD-281, #466).
