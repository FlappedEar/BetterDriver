Alpha 0.3.19: G-forces from the RaceBox and the phone together. Like RaceChrono, the app now averages the RaceBox's accelerometer and the phone's own, so the G-G diagram, braking and the video overlay read the car's G more accurately.

What changes for the driver:
- **Both accelerometers count.** Each one's mount is found from GPS (turned, tilted or on its side), then forward and sideways G are averaged from every accelerometer that agrees with GPS. The steadier one counts for more. Before, only the first one that agreed was used and the other was ignored.
- **The phone's clock is matched to the RaceBox's** before averaging (up to 0.3 s apart), so braking points stay sharp.
- **A phone that moves in its holder is left out.** If its reading does not agree with the RaceBox's, it is not averaged in.
- **Gaps are filled.** Where the RaceBox skips a sample or drops the link, the phone carries on.
- **The video overlay shows the same G as the analysis.** Before, it used the RaceBox's X and Y axes alone with no mount fit and no phone. On the 2026-10-13 drive (new RaceBox Mini, mount turned) that made sideways G come from GPS.
- **The debug log says how.** The `AccelFit` line gives each accelerometer's share and clock offset, e.g. `forward from accel_x 0.64 + phone_accel_y 0.36 (+0.05 s)`.
