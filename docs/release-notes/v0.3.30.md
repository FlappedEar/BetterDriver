Alpha 0.3.30: fixes from the 2026-10-17 afternoon drive (2 h on the road, RaceBox Micro, phone on battery).

What changes for the driver:
- **Video no longer stops at Android's "emergency".** On the drive Android reported emergency for 12 min at a battery of only 46.6–47.5 °C, while the phone kept recording 1080p · 60 without a fault. 0.3.29 would have stopped video there and lost the last 27 min. Video now stops only when the battery reaches 60 °C or Android shuts the phone down for heat (or at "critical" if the phone gives no battery temperature).
- **Live screen and coach keep getting fixes when the phone's storage is slow.** Live data used to wait until it was saved to disk. When a 4K file ended with storage nearly full, a 2.2 s save stall showed the "no GPS" banner while every fix was on time. Live data now goes to the screen and the coach as it arrives; saving is unchanged.
- **Video lines up more reliably.** A phone video file used to be placed by its single lowest timing report. Under heat, single reports came up to 7 frames early and shifted a file by 100 ms. A file is now placed by a value that at least three reports from its first minute agree on. Sessions already recorded keep their placement.
- **Long session exports no longer end the app.** A 63-minute drive's VBO export ran out of the 256 MB heap. Session data is now packed, the G columns read only GNSS and accelerometers, the app asks for a large heap, an out-of-memory export ends as Export failed, and the start log line records the heap limit (FBBD-232, #190).
- Tip: 4K · 60 uses about 36 GB an hour; on the drive it filled the phone after 47 min (video stops below 1 GB free, data carries on). 1080p · 60 uses about a quarter of that.
