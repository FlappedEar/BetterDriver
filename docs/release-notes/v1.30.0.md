1.30.0: the iPhone and iPad take Android's Health-like look, the same drive is no longer listed twice, lap review plays two videos smoothly on iPad and Mac, video with data plays on after a seek on the iPhone, track editor lines fit the track's real width, saved perfect-lap videos keep MapTiler's photo, and Google's satellite photos stay inside the free tier.

What changes for the driver:
- iPhone and iPad look as Android since 1.28.0: dark cards with amber headings, the amber tab bar with Android's icons, Drive's Pinned cards (Next session here, Last time here, This session), Sessions as day titles with a Track day card and session cards, and the Summary's Pinned cards and Highlights (FBBD-421, #502).
- Importing a VBO or RaceChrono CSV of a drive that is already here is refused, and at start the app removes extra imported copies of the same drive (never a recorded session, never one with video, always keeping one), on Android and iOS (FBBD-427, #507).
- Lap review plays two videos smoothly on iPad and Mac: the players no longer change speed every frame, and only the cursors redraw (FBBD-429, #508).
- iPhone and iPad: a video with data plays on at once after a seek, also after seeking back on iOS 27, and the iPad keeps its full window after a video (FBBD-435, #517).
- Track editor: a line placed from the lap is fitted to every pass of the drive at that point, 12 to 30 m wide instead of 30 m, and Width −/+ changes it in 2 m steps, on Android and iOS (FBBD-438, #516).
- Saved perfect-lap videos show MapTiler's satellite photo with its credit when a MapTiler key is entered (Google's and Apple's terms do not allow their photos in a saved video); without one the road is drawn and a note says why (FBBD-433, #511).
- Google satellite photos stop at 2,500 tiles a day per device, inside Google's free tier; the map then shows MapTiler, OpenStreetMap or Apple until the next day. On Android, watching more laps of a track no longer fetches Google's tiles again (FBBD-412, #510).
