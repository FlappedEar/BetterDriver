1.33.7: the day before Moto Park, the fixes from the 7 Nov pre-track audit: phone video sound with the coach, the session's video on file errors, storage, and the coach at a new track.

What changes for the driver:
- iPhone: phone video keeps its sound after the coach speaks; the coach voice no longer turns the audio session off while the camera records sound. With the coach on and the camera recording away from a saved track, a voice check is said 30 s after START and every 2 min, and each phrase logs the audio session and the camera's sound 2 s later (FBBD-466, #572).
- iPhone: a coach phrase asked for after STOP is not said (audit BD-T09, FBBD-466, #572).
- iPhone: the START sheet says how long the chosen video mode fits in the free space; the track screen warns with minutes of video left under 10 min (FBBD-467, #573).
- iPhone/iPad: a session shared by AirDrop, an export and a video with data no longer leave a full copy behind once sent or saved; the iPad removes the received file after import; leftovers are cleared at launch (FBBD-467, #573).
- iPhone: an error opening a phone video file is retried (0, 2, 4 ... 30 s) instead of ending the session's video; the track screen says "Phone video stopped" while it is stopped; every video finish runs as a background task (FBBD-468, #575).
- iPhone: the first session at a new track feeds the coach for the track made from it (FBBD-469, #574).
- iPhone: the drive log records each camera switch and video-mode choice on the START sheet (FBBD-463, #571).
- Android: unchanged since 1.33.5.

Not in this build: phone video file rotation every 5 min (built in #575, off until after Moto Park); a test of the coach speaking with a real camera and microphone (the simulator has no camera).
