Alpha 0.3.23: GNSS data put back at the time of its fix (FBBD-229).

What changes for the driver:
- **Position and speed line up with the video and G again.** The RaceBox sends each GNSS fix together with its current acceleration, and the fix is older. On the 2026-10-16 drive (two RaceBoxes, one in RaceChrono) the speed changed 0.8 s after both the RaceBox's and the phone's accelerometers felt it, and the 2026-10-13 drive showed the same. Positions and speeds were stamped 0.8 s late against the video, the G-forces and the phone. When a session is read, the app now measures how old the fix is from the RaceBox's own accelerometer (only while driving, for at least a minute) and puts the GNSS values back at their time. This applies to analysis, the video overlay, Video with data and every export (VBO, VBO + video, CSV, GPX), and to sessions already recorded. Lap times do not change, since every crossing moves by the same amount.
- Not changed yet: the live track screen still gets each fix when it arrives, about 0.8 s after it was taken.
