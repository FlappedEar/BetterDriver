1.37.0: coach advice from your usual pass at a corner, not one odd lap; the first steps of the learning coach (brake-to-throttle gap, exercises and their result), not yet shown on screen.

What changes for the driver:
- The coach: a corner cue now asks for what your usual pass at that corner needs (the median of its last 3 race-pace passes that lost time, within 3 laps), and only if the last pass still needs it too. One pass that braked early by accident no longer gets "brake later", and a change you made on the last lap is not asked for again (FBBD-410, #621, 7ea4ec3).
- Learning coach: after each analysed session the app measures the brake-to-throttle gap (time off both pedals between releasing the brake and pressing the accelerator) at every corner and lap, and flags a corner where it is long on most laps compared with the session's other corners. It needs measured pedals (FR-S CAN or OBD); without them it is off and says why. It writes `LearningCoach` lines to the app log; there is no screen for it yet (FBBD-417, #620, 9bcbd56).
- Learning coach: from those flags and the session focus, the app proposes up to 3 exercises per session, and the coach can coach a picked exercise's corner only ("Turn 9, brake to throttle"). There is no screen to pick an exercise yet (UI round 67), so until then nothing is picked and the coach behaves as before (FBBD-418, #622, a5b0087).
- Learning coach: after a session in which the coach worked on a picked exercise, the app compares that corner's brake-to-throttle gap and its corner-plus-exit-straight time with the exercise's baseline and keeps the result (not enough data, not done, done without gain, done and faster), logged as `exercise.result`; when the gap stayed, one optional question (traffic, car moving, on purpose, not sure) can keep that exercise out of the next proposals. The result card and the question wait for UI round 68 (FBBD-419, #624, 63a3184).
- Behind the scenes: the coach's "still needed" check covers the new brake-to-throttle advice, which the merge of #621 and #622 had missed (#623, 7d1b8a3d); TestFlight builds with Xcode 27 and the iOS 27 SDK, as App Store Connect asks (FBBD-487, #619, 9bbbbd3; first shipped as TestFlight 1618 of 1.36.0).
