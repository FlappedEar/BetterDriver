1.10.0: the getting-faster loop. Each session gets a line plan with where to find time per corner (FBBD-306), the live coach aims at that plan and says the line and the throttle (FBBD-307), the next session proves what was won back (FBBD-310, FBBD-313), the perfect lap plays in 3D and saves as a video (FBBD-308), and the whole loop runs in CI on the real Jastrząb and Silesia Ring days (FBBD-311).

What changes for the driver:
- The Map tab of a session has a second view, Where to find time (UI-167 B): the whole lap with the time to find per corner and a list ranked by it (UI-164 A). A corner opened from the list zooms the map to it with the road the laps used, his lap coloured by pedals and the target as a thin green line (UI-165 C), and says what to do there in words (UI-166 A). Phone and tablet (#310, #311).
- The target in each corner is the second fastest pass over every session at the track, the coach's rule; less than 0.05 s is left out as timing noise. One line plan per track is kept from the latest analysed session (#311).
- "▶ Perfect lap in 3D" on Where to find time plays the plan's perfect lap from above each corner, with the best lap as a ghost, play/pause, 1x/half speed, corner jumps and a HUD (UI-180..184). Save video writes a 1920x1080 30 fps MP4 to Movies/BetterDriver, with a Send button (#317).
- The live coach aims at the line plan's target lap in every corner until the session beats it twice (#315).
- The coach now also says the line ("Turn 1, tighter at the apex") and the throttle point ("Turn 1, throttle 5 after the apex", only with OBD throttle), up to two things per cue (UI-168 B, UI-169 C). Corners taken on a lift count (#313, #318).
- After a cued corner the coach line shows what came of it: green "Apex 1.8 m tighter ✓ · −0.09 s" or grey "not yet" (UI-170 B).
- The out-lap gets the plan once: "Today, turns 9, 1 and 4", and the coach line shows "Today: T9 · T1 · T4 · ≈ 1.0 s to find" (UI-173 A, UI-174 A).
- A third track screen shows the map of the next corner (#318).
- The next-session card links to Where to find time with "≈ N s ›" (UI-190 B). Last focus says the time won back in the focus corners, the time left to the target and whether the coach spoke there (UI-191 A, #316). It judges the plan corners the coach worked on (#319).
- A lap with no other cue gets its lap time before any praise.
- CI runs the getting-faster loop over the six Jastrząb sessions of 29 Aug and three Silesia Ring sessions and fails when a link breaks (#312, #314).
