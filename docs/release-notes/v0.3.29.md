Alpha 0.3.29: phone video keeps your chosen quality in the heat, from the 2026-10-17 heat stress test.

What changes for the driver:
- **No more heat step-downs.** Video keeps the mode you chose (for example 4K · 60) for the whole session. Arek's heat test of 2026-10-17 showed Android calling the phone "critical" at a battery of only 41–45 °C while it recorded 4K · 60 for 55 min without a fault; the old policy would have cut video at 6.5 min. Video now stops only when the battery reaches 60 °C or Android itself signals a thermal emergency or shutdown; data recording carries on either way. The thermal log line now also shows the battery temperature.
- **No empty video file on a hot start.** Thermal checks wait until the file has recorded media; Android's first thermal call right after the start no longer stops a file before it holds a frame (CameraX error 8, seen 2026-10-17).
- **Quality report:** Android's severe or critical label no longer fails a session. The thermal check passes when video has stopped by the time the battery reads 60 °C, and fails a thermal shutdown.
- Settings › Diagnostics › Ignore phone temperature still exists for stress tests; with it on, even the 60 °C stop is skipped.
