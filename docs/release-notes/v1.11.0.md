What changes for the driver:
- Sessions has a "Track day ›" link in a day's heading when a track was driven in two or more sessions that day (UI-210 B).
- The track day page shows the day's numbers and a chart of its sessions (UI-211 A), each corner in words with its kind and a small trend line (UI-212 A, UI-213 A), and what to do next time there and what came of the last day (UI-214 B) (#322).
- The day's targets come from the corners of the day's fastest lap: per corner the second fastest pass of the day, what the best lap and a typical lap lose there, and what to do (#322).
- The first session at that track on the next day gets the day's three corners as its coach focus; later sessions use the line plan as before (UI-214 B, #322).
- The perfect lap in 3D follows the car from behind and above (UI-200 A), over a MapTiler satellite photo when the track editor has a key, else the drawn track (UI-201 B).
- The line is drawn as a car-wide band coloured by the pedals with BRAKE and GAS bars across the road (UI-202 C); the car and speed look as before (UI-203 B); the controls sit in one bar on the picture and Save video moves to the header (UI-204 A). The saved video fetches its own tiles and carries MapTiler's credit (#323).
- The import card shows the file's name, "640 MB of 1.6 GB · 40 %" and the time left (UI-192 B). One pick can import several files, oldest session first, with a bar for all files above the current one (UI-193 A); a file that fails does not stop the rest, and the end lists each result. The card stays on top of Sessions and Tracks (UI-194 A) (#321).
- The import bar no longer stays at 0 % when the file's provider gives no size; a file of unknown size shows a moving bar with the bytes read (#321).
