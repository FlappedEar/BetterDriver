Alpha 0.3.6: the build for the validation drive, with the drive checks graded correctly and the fixes from Codex's review of 17 PRs.

What changes for the driver:
- **Video drift graded correctly (FBBD-213).** On a video file longer than 30 min, drift is now measured over the time between the two readings it compares. A drift just over 100 ms per 30 min now fails instead of passing.
- **Timing and hardware fixes (FBBD-210).**
  - RaceBox samples arriving across the GPS week change are stamped at the right time, not a week ahead.
  - A GoPro clip recorded on local time is placed at the right time, not two hours late.
  - A circuit refresh interrupted by a recording is retried.
  - The coach never takes a full stop for a corner.
  - The headset delay test needs the headset connected the whole time.
- **Corner numbers and the data quality card (FBBD-211).**
  - An older session no longer replaces a track's current corner layout.
  - A thermal shutdown fails the session's quality report.
  - RaceChrono OBD speed is labelled as OBD, not GPS.
- **Privacy.** Test data in the public repository no longer shows where Arek's road drives start and end (FBBD-194).
- **New automated checks (FBBD-196, FBBD-197).**
  - Screenshot tests on phone and tablet.
  - Track-screen contrast of at least 7:1 for sunlight.
  - Video review sync tests.
  - Lap times are no longer clipped in a short window (FBBD-40).
