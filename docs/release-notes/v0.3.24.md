Alpha 0.3.24: the live coach allows for the late GNSS fix (FBBD-229).

What changes for the driver:
- **Corner cues come early enough again.** The RaceBox's fix arrives about 0.8 s after it was taken, so the coach planned each cue from where the car had been 0.8 s earlier. That is about 30 m at 130 km/h, and a cue could run into the braking zone. While recording, the app now measures how old the fixes are from the RaceBox's own accelerometer: at least a minute of driving, measured again every 30 s over the last 3 minutes. The coach takes that time off its time to the next braking zone. The debug log records each value as "gnss fix age … ms". Until the first measurement, usually during the first lap, cues are timed as before.
- The live track screen's lap time, sectors and delta were already right. Every fix is late by the same amount, so the start of the lap and the time at each point move together. They still update about 0.8 s after the moment they show.
- Sessions read back after recording are corrected as in 0.3.23.
