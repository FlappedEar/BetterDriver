Alpha 0.3.25: a better map in the track editor (FBBD-230).

What changes for the driver:
- **MapTiler satellite map.** Open a track in the editor, tap the "Map" button at the bottom right of the map, choose "Add MapTiler key…" and paste your MapTiler key. From then on the editor shows MapTiler's satellite imagery everywhere, loaded from MapTiler's servers in sharp 512 px tiles. Do it once on the phone and once on the tablet. The key stays on the device: it is not in the repository or the APK.
- **Pick the map.** The same button switches between MapTiler satellite, the GUGiK orthophoto (Poland only) and OpenStreetMap.
- **The GUGiK orthophoto loads faster.** Each request now brings a 512 px image, a quarter of the requests for the same sharpness.
- **No more dark squares while panning.** Until a tile arrives, the nearest coarser tile already loaded stands in, up to four levels up.
- The editor is the only map with imagery. Lap review, compare and analysis draw the track line on a plain background, as before.
