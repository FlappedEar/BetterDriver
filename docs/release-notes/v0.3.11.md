Alpha 0.3.11: two 1.0 readiness fixes (FBBD-220). Use this build instead of 0.3.10 for the next drive and the headset test, so the drive log also measures start-up time and app freezes.

What changes for the driver:
- **Cue review numbers only when they are trustworthy (FBBD-177).** After a session, a cue's result shows metres, km/h and seconds only when the cue and the pass after it are both High confidence. Otherwise the same sentence appears without the numbers, for example "Braked later, short of best · time gained". Results recorded by older builds show no numbers.
- **Start-up time and app freezes in the drive log (FBBD-41).** The diagnostic log records how long the app takes to open from cold and every time Android ended the app (freeze, crash or other), read at start and when a recording ends. Each recording's quality report adds a Responsiveness section: cold start at most 1.5 s, and no freezes (ANRs) during the recording, or "not measured" with the reason.
