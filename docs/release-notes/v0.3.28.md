Alpha 0.3.28: a switch to ignore the phone's temperature, for a heat stress test.

What changes for the driver:
- **Settings › Diagnostics › Ignore phone temperature** (off by default). While it is on, phone video keeps the quality you chose however hot the phone gets: no step-down, no "Stopped: too hot". Only the phone itself may throttle or shut the camera. It applies from the next session and stays on until you switch it off.
- **The log keeps the whole stress test.** While it is on, every 5 s camera thermal reading (status, headroom) goes to the log, next to the 30 s health line (battery temperature, current, thermal status, headroom), camera errors, crashes and ANRs. The session's quality report then fails only a thermal shutdown, and notes that the temperature was ignored.
