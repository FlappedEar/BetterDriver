Alpha 0.3.35: the Demo track can drive one of your own sessions again, the demo circuit gives coach cues sooner, and the track screen shows the lap number with the current lap time.

What changes for the driver:
- **Demo track › Drives** (FBBD-235, UI-78 A): choose what the made-up car drives, either the Demo circuit or a session on the phone, Silesia Ring for example. Only the session's timed laps are replayed, one after another, at its saved track. A session without a saved track, without two timed laps, or at a point-to-point track falls back to the Demo circuit.
- **The Demo circuit now makes mistakes**: now and then a corner is braked too early or taken too slowly, so the coach has something to say from about 5 minutes in instead of lap 10.
- **Add equipment**: Demo GNSS and Demo track are now in their own **Try the app** group, below the rest (UI-79 B). The Demo track page note says what Drives does (UI-81 B).
- **Track screen lap times block** (FBBD-236, UI-82): the lap number shows large next to the running time of the current lap, which replaces the predicted lap time. The Lap tile is gone, so the tiles default to Speed (with G when the receiver gives it).
- Recording, video, lap timing at real tracks, heat and the quality report are unchanged from 0.3.34.
