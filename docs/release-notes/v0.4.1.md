Alpha 0.4.1: phone video stops only at Android's thermal shutdown, and the session report says nothing about heat unless a shutdown happened (FBBD-250).

What changes for the driver:
- Phone video no longer stops at a 60 °C battery or at Android's critical status. It stops only when Android reports thermal shutdown (status 6), and then it finishes the file first. This holds with Ignore phone temperature on or off.
- The session report has no thermal rows any more: no thermal status, no battery temperature, no soak thermal or battery rows. Only an Android thermal shutdown adds a Thermal shutdown section, which fails and checks that no phone video file was left unfinished. Stored reports are made again in the new format.
- The video mode dialog no longer talks about heat.
- Lap timing and the coach are unchanged from 0.4.0.
