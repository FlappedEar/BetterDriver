Alpha 0.3.2: everything merged since 0.3.1, checked by the new release gate.

What changes for the driver:
- Each session has a **Data quality** card in its lap analysis: GNSS rate, dropped samples, gaps and reconnects, recorder restarts, video coverage, cue delays and thermal steps, each marked pass or fail against the validation targets (FBBD-183).
- RaceBox samples are timed from the receiver's GNSS clock instead of Bluetooth arrival, removing up to 99 ms of video sync jitter. Longer RaceBox messages are now read (FBBD-170, FBBD-182).
- The exported VBO no longer names RaceBox, so Circuit Tools no longer reports "not genuine RACEBOX VBO file" (FBBD-184).
- Session import from VBO, GPX and RaceChrono CSV (FBBD-164), and an optimal-lap video stitched from the theoretical best (FBBD-163).
- Track screen: full brightness and a faster GNSS-lost banner with an optional tone (FBBD-63, FBBD-64).
- Coach voice: audio focus, pre-rendered cue clips and a headset delay test (FBBD-47, FBBD-48, FBBD-49). The coach reference also uses sessions recorded before their track was saved (FBBD-168).
- Tablet layouts with a navigation rail and the session list beside the analysis (FBBD-40).
- Point-to-point sessions get corners and segments (FBBD-162).
- Fixes: removing a device or a car no longer also closes Settings; the lap review stops drawing while paused (FBBD-169); contrast on the loss colour (FBBD-62).
