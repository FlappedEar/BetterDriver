1.6.3: a heart rate strap that is not worn no longer shows or records a made-up heart rate (FBBD-285).

What changes for the driver:
- A listed strap without 5 plausible heart rates in a row in the first 45 s of a session is switched off until the session ends. On 21 Oct a strap lying on a cable sent 0 to 37 bpm for 9 min and the track screen showed 24; such a strap is now never used. The session report says it was off and why.
- Heart rates outside 40 to 230 bpm are treated like a strap without skin contact: not shown on the track screen and not recorded, RR-Intervals included. The log names the first such value and the strap's flags.
