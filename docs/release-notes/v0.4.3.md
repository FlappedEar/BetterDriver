Alpha 0.4.3: 2.7K·60 phone video records through BetterDriver's own encoder instead of Android's MediaRecorder (FBBD-246), and a full 2.7K file hands over to the next without a gap or a fallback to 1080p (FBBD-248).

What changes for the driver:
- 2.7K·60 phone video goes from the camera straight into a video encoder the app runs itself (MediaCodec, at real-time priority, asked for twice the frame rate), and the app writes the MP4 files itself (MediaMuxer). In 0.4.0 MediaRecorder kept 49.8 fps: the camera skipped 8,125 of 50,364 frames (16 %) while the encoder held its buffers. The cause is inferred, with medium confidence; the next 2.7K log confirms it.
- When a 2.7K file nears 4 GB, the next file starts at a keyframe one frame after the last one of the file before, with sound on both sides. In 0.4.0 the handover at 3.8 GB failed, the next file was deleted and the session fell back to 1080p·60 with a ~0.6 s gap.
- The log line for each 2.7K file adds camera= (frames the camera delivered) and encode_ms= (encoder time, p50/p95/max), and the log names the encoder in use, so the next log shows whether the camera or the encoder holds frames back.
- The video encoder is H.264; HEVC only on a phone whose H.264 encoder cannot keep up with 2.7K·60 and whose HEVC encoder can.
- A file that could not be finished after its handover is removed from the session's clip list.
- Lap timing and the coach are unchanged from 0.4.1.
