Alpha 0.3.37: the Demo track can drive Arek's Silesia Ring laps from 27 Sep 2026, built into the app, with no session on the phone.

What changes for the driver:
- **Demo track › Drives** (FBBD-237, FBBD-238): a new choice, "Sep 27, 2026 … · Silesia Ring" (date and time in the phone's format), drives the timed laps of the three Silesia Ring sessions of 27 Sep 2026 (19 laps, 134 to 200 s), one after another, at the Silesia Ring track. - Drives still lists the Demo circuit and the sessions on the phone. An empty list in 0.3.35 meant there were no sessions on the phone; the log now says how many sessions Drives found and logs each session delete.
- Recording, video, lap timing at real tracks, heat and the quality report are unchanged from 0.3.36.
