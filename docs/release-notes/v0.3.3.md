Alpha 0.3.3: everything merged since 0.3.2, mostly fixes, checked by the release gate.

What changes for the driver:
- **Coach on a first outing:** corners now come from real braking, never from a stop past the line, so lap times are no longer read out while you brake for Turn 1. The coach also goes quiet on the in-lap and in the pit lane (FBBD-202).
- **Coach warm-up:** when you go back out on the same day, the coach waits at most one timed lap, instead of the learning laps (FBBD-190).
- **Corner numbers:** each track keeps lasting corner numbers, so "Turn 3" means the same corner in the analysis, the coach and the video (FBBD-172).
- **Where values come from:** every value in the lap analysis that is not measured (calculated, inferred or simulated) is marked "≈", with a legend. Imports keep their receiver and rate, and the Data quality card lists each channel's source (FBBD-174).
- **GoPro:** clips of short sessions are no longer dropped when the GoPro's clock is a little off (FBBD-201).
- **Mock session:** a mock Silesia Ring session with video can be imported for trying tablet review and coaching between track days (FBBD-191).
- **Fixes from code review:**
  - RaceBox messages arriving together are stamped at their own times.
  - The missing-message count is right below 25 Hz.
  - VBO channel units carry no RaceBox text.
  - A finished phone video can no longer stop the next session's camera.
  - The GNSS rate is right after a gap.
  - The recorder restarts correctly on Android 14.
  - Backup restore reloads tracks and settings and blocks recording while it runs.
  - BOM-prefixed VBOs keep their date.
  - Short sprints get a distance axis.
  - A malformed clip is left out of video export (FBBD-185..189, FBBD-198..200).
