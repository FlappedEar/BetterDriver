Alpha 0.3.43: the coach speaks Polish, with any speech engine on the phone (FBBD-245).

What changes for the driver:
- **Settings › App › Spoken coach** has two new rows above Voice (Arek's picks UI-96 A, UI-101 A):
  - **Language**: English or Polski.
  - **Speech engine**: any text-to-speech engine installed on the phone, for example SherpaTTS with Piper neural voices from F-Droid, instead of Google's.
- The Voice page lists only the chosen engine's offline voices of the chosen language, for example "Polski · Voice 2". Picking another engine starts from that engine's own voice.
- In Polish every cue is said in Polish, live or from pre-rendered clips:
  - Corners 1 to 10 are named by their number nouns, and from 11 on as "Zakręt 11" (UI-97 B). Phrases are short, like the English ones (UI-98 A): "Trójka, hamuj na 100, 10 później", "Trójka, 60 w apeksie, 5 więcej", "Trójka, to zadziałało".
  - Lap times are the bare numbers: "1, 32, 4" (UI-99 B with Arek's note).
  - Numbers are read in Polish ("sto pięćdziesiąt"), with metr, metry or metrów as the number needs.
- The coach line on the track screen and the Coach review follow the spoken language (UI-100 B), for example "Hamowanie na 108 m, 8 m później niż poprzednio · zysk 0,12 s". The rest of the app stays in English.
- Test voice and the voice samples say the sample cue in the chosen language.
- English cues are unchanged.
- Recording, lap timing, video and analysis are unchanged from 0.3.42.
