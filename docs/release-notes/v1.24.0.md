1.24.0: a Video tab on Android, and the iPhone catches up with the Demo track and the coaching history.

What changes for the driver:
- An Android session with video gets a seventh tab, Video, after Car, as on the iPhone and iPad: pick the whole session, a composite lap or a lap with its seconds before and after, then Watch it with the data band or Save video (FBBD-392, #453).
- On the iPhone, Settings › Equipment › Add equipment › Try the app › Demo track drives the same made-up circuit and built-in Silesia Ring and Jastrząb replays as Android, from the START sheet like a receiver (FBBD-373, #457).
- The iPhone and iPad Summary shows Android's "Last focus · what came of it" card under the lap times: whether the last focus came off, the time won back through the line plans, and each row opens every pass through the corner (FBBD-364, #458).
- A heart-rate strap on the iPhone now retries, waits and restarts its link exactly as on Android, from one shared link core (FBBD-358, #455).
- The iPhone's RaceBox link runs the same shared link core with Android's RaceBox numbers: retries every 0.5 to 3 s, the setup ladder, a restart after 3 s without data, and the same Link lines in the log (FBBD-358, #459).
- The Summary's card against earlier sessions at the track reads those sessions in the shared core on both platforms; it looks the same as before (FBBD-377, #454).

Not in this release: the iPhone and iPad Data quality card (FBBD-365, #456); it goes in the next release.

## Acceptance
- Recording: PASS - JVM DemoTrackTest and ScriptFeedTest, and the iOS DemoTrackTests UI test records the Jastrząb replay until a lap is timed and opens the session.
- Timing: PASS - JavaRandomTest keeps the Demo circuit's laps unchanged after the move to the shared core; DemoTrackCoreTest times laps in a LiveSession on iOS.
- Video: PASS - VideoTabSmokeTest (emulator) opens the Video tab of a session with video, finds Save video enabled and plays the best lap with Watch; a session without video has no Video tab.
- Analysis: PASS - the shared readEarlierSessions and progress tests run on JVM and iOS (#454); ProofUiTest moved to commonTest and runs on iOS, with iOS tests for kept coaching records (#458).
- Coach: PASS - the coaching history and won-back figure come from the shared CoachingRows, tested on both platforms (#458).
- Storage: PASS - iOS tests for kept coaching records, progression read-back and track delete (#458).
- Hardware: NOT RUN - the shared heart-rate and RaceBox links (#455, #459) have NotifyLinkCoreTest unit tests only; not yet tried with a strap or a RaceBox on the iPhone.
