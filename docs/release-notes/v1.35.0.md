1.35.0: phone video in 5-minute files, a calmer, steadier coach, in and out laps kept out of review, a phone camera that never gives up on Android, and data fixes from the 1.34.1 code review.

What changes for the driver:
- The coach: no corner cue when the time lost there is within the corner's usual spread from lap to lap (it was a "brake a little later" said from noise). Each held-back cue is logged as `coach_skipped` with its reason (FBBD-414, #588, 31d8d8e).
- The coach: the line plan's target lap is found by its start time, not by lap time alone, and the coach aims only at a pass you have driven at least twice (code review F4, F5; #606, 6f4a12f).
- The coach: each cue logs how long it was expected to take to say (`say_ms`), and the session check reports how far the real sound was from it per language (FBBD-406, #608, 6bc9fb0).
- Analysis (Android and iPhone/iPad): a lap through the pit lane is an in lap and the lap after it an out lap, as in live timing. They are no longer ranked as best lap or used as the coach's reference (FBBD-482, #600, 907f8ea).
- Android: the phone camera keeps retrying after an error for the whole session (at once, then 2, 4, 8 ... 30 s), as on iPhone. A "Phone video stopped" alert shows on the track screen, also when the camera process dies mid-recording; the lap review writes a `LapReview` log line (FBBD-442, FBBD-470, FBBD-455; #589, 6399c27).
- Phone video (both apps): a new file starts every 5 minutes, so a killed app, a crash or a dead phone loses at most the open file instead of the whole session's video. Android's own encoder cuts at the first keyframe after 5 minutes (FBBD-468, #610, 6687e27).
- Recording (both apps): a STOP whose end could not be written says so ("a write failed; what was written is kept and recovered at the next start") instead of "saved"; on the iPhone a low-space stop in the middle of a batch counts the unwritten rest as dropped (FBBD-478, #599, f2623e6).
- iPhone: Share waits up to 60 s for the phone video to finish after STOP, so a shared session never carries a video without its index (FBBD-477, #598, 61cb3e8).
- Android: the RaceBox / Bluetooth GPS link runs on the same shared connection core as the iPhone; a setup that gets no data names the step it was waiting on in the log (FBBD-358, #592, dd08aee).
- Android: a GoPro download that fails leaves no `.part` file behind (FBBD-480, #594, e013884).
- iPhone and iPad: a restore that failed after its point of no return closes the app to a "Restore unfinished" screen with Try again, as on Android, so a track saved in between can no longer be lost (FBBD-476, #597, 8a91f8d).
- iPhone and iPad: after Clear log, the next shared log holds nothing from before the clear, and the system-log part is capped at 20,000 lines (FBBD-483, #601, 99b56e3).
- iPhone: a session lists every source it recorded (receiver, phone IMU, OBD, heart rate, TPMS), as on Android. VBO export (both apps) leaves out a cached fix from more than 2 s before the session start (FBBD-436, #591, 41a80bf).
- Import (both apps): a VBO speed column in knots is converted from knots, and each acceleration axis uses its own unit (FBBD-479, #593, 9a07876).
- About (both apps): all four bundled fonts are credited (FBBD-484, #602, b38e545).
- iPhone: the Demo track's clock stops when the recorder ends a recording by itself (storage full or a write error) (FBBD-484, #603, e34358c).
- Behind the scenes: the Android and iPhone recorders share one set of writing rules (chunk rotation, free-space check, dropped-sample report), with no change in behaviour (FBBD-360, #607, 71cab09); Guava 33.7.2-android, outside CVE-2026-102554 (FBBD-484, #604, 3aa0696); Compose screen effects return to the main thread after background work (FBBD-485, #609, d1c5f1e); a test that failed outside UTC (FBBD-484, #595, d33b5d9); CI and TestFlight gates (FBBD-481, #596, 4ae4fc6; FBBD-457, #587, f098077; #605, eba58c9: demo receiver records no weather); iOS CI flakes (FBBD-465, #590, e7381fb).
