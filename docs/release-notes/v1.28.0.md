1.28.0: a new Apple Health-like look on Android with Pinned and Highlights at the top of a session's Summary, the realistic optimal and theoretical best can be watched in the Video tab, tablets only review sessions, every session can record tyre pressures from the TPMS bridge and keeps the weather at the track, the coach is more careful before the first track day, and the iPhone recorder no longer makes sensors wait for the disk.

What changes for the driver:
- The realistic optimal and the raw theoretical best can now be watched in a session's Video tab, on Android, iPhone and iPad, with their own data band naming the lap each part comes from; before they could only be saved (FBBD-402, #486).
- A tablet (iPad or Android) now only reviews: its tabs are Sessions and Settings, Settings keeps Cars, Tracks, App and Diagnostics, and a car's page shows its name and notes. The OBD-II link, equipment, track screen, video recording and spoken coach setup stay on the phone that records. All recorded data stays in a session's review (#487).
- Tyre pressures and temperatures from the FlappedEar TPMS bridge (RCTPMS): add "Tyre pressure bridge" under Equipment; every session then records each tyre's pressure (bar) and temperature (°C) about once a second, on Android and the iPhone. They go into the VBO export (tyre_pressure_fl..rr, tyre_temp_fl..rr) and the video's Tyres widget. A wheel whose reading has not changed for 5 min is left out until it changes; a lost bridge never stops the session or the GPS. Not yet run with the real bridge (FBBD-224, #489).
- Each session keeps the weather at the track (air temperature, wind and gusts, rain, dry/damp/wet surface) from Open-Meteo, shown as a Weather card in the Summary on phone and tablet, with a "Last time here" comparison against the previous session at the same place. On a damp or wet track the spoken coach says lap times only, since the dry reference no longer applies. Weather data by Open-Meteo.com (CC BY 4.0) (FBBD-403, #490).
- The spoken coach on a first visit to a track (every session there from today) aims only at what was already driven, never at braking points beyond the best; a cue whose sound was cut off earns no result or praise; the time a cue needs follows the speech rate setting; at a track without an earlier reference a faster clean lap during the learning laps sets the corners again; and a line plan target that matches two laps of a session gives no aim instead of a guess (FBBD-415, FBBD-416, FBBD-406, FBBD-409, FBBD-405 part, #492).
- Android: a new look outside the car, like Apple Health: true black with dark grey cards, amber headings and the Inter font, large titles, and Drive and Sessions laid out as cards. A session's Summary now opens with Pinned (next session, best actual lap with lap bars, heart rate per lap), then Highlights (the typical lap against the session before, in one sentence), then the lap times and the rest as before. The track screen is unchanged; the iPhone and iPad follow later (UI-307 A, UI-308 A, FBBD-317, #494; iOS FBBD-421).
- Android: the last video file's sound now runs to the moment of the stop; before, up to about 0.1 s of sound at the end could be cut off (FBBD-422, #496).
- iPhone: sensors and GPS no longer wait for the disk; a write queue saves the session, the stop logs how the writer kept up ("writer: max_wait_ms=N max_write_ms=N max_queue=N dropped=N"), and phone video is written in 2 s fragments so a file cut off by a killed app still plays (FBBD-350, #476).
