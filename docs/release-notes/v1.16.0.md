1.16.0: the track screen's tiles are set in Settings, one layout per car (FBBD-345), and the RaceBox link no longer drops for 0.6-0.8 s after a needless fast-link re-request (FBBD-305). Batched so Arek can test what is on main before the 29 Oct drive.

What changes for the driver:
- **Settings › Track screen** draws both track screens as they look in the car; tap a tile to pick what it shows, or **Reset tiles to default**. Each car has its own layout, picked under **Layout for**; a car without its own starts from the tiles set so far (#389).
- The tile list offers the FR-S CAN channels (steering angle, brake pressure, brake) before the car has sent them (#389).
- On the track screen the 1 s hold on a tile is gone; the only gesture left is the 3 s hold to stop (#389).
- The app no longer asks the RaceBox for the fast link again when the link already runs at 15 ms on 2M. On the 28 Oct 20:04 FR-S drive each of the three 640-760 ms RaceBox gaps began just after such a re-request (#392).
- The log now shows the OBD adapter's Bluetooth connection interval and MTU, so the next drive shows why the FR-S CAN rate differed between two drives (#392).
- iOS app work on main (TestFlight builds, not part of the Android APK): GoPro files added to a session and lined up by the GoPro's GPS (#384). The MP4/GPMF readers moved to shared Kotlin; Android output is unchanged.
